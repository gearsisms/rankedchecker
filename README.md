Ranked Checker helps you see your ranked standings at any point with additional defensive trophies included. This gives a better idea of where the group stands, since the in-game leaderboard does not add these in until after the week finishes. To give this tool data, you need to paste two tables from clashspot into it. The tool walks you through how to acquire these tables. Next, you can adjust the settings to meet your needs. It will then provide the updated rankings and some other cool stats. It does not work well with mobile.
