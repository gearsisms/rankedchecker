Ranked
Reshuffle Clash of Clans ranked weeks are decided after the last attack. Unused defences pay out at your own average — so the table you see is not the table you finish in.

avg = defence trophies / defences
bonus = avg × (battles − defences)
final = trophies + bonus  (shown rounded down)

2Copy each table in

On each page: select everything (Ctrl+A, or ⌘+A on a Mac), copy, and paste it into the matching box. Grab the whole page — the menus, the footer and the small "best battle of the week" boxes are all filtered out, so there is no need to select the table carefully.

Faster way, if you don't mind one bit of setup
Grab standings

Drag Grab standings onto your bookmarks bar. Then one click on any group page copies everything at once — including player tags and Town Hall levels, which the copy-paste route can't see. Paste the result below.

2Settings

Picking a league fills in the battle count and the promotion and demotion cutoffs for you; both stay editable. Trophies display rounded down, the way the in-game leaderboard shows them, but placements are decided on the exact value including decimals.
